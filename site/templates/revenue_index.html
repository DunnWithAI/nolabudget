Other funds

Besides the General Fund, the city budgets {{ other.tot_new|money }} in {{ py }} from other funds, {{ fmt.change(other.tot_new, other.tot_old) }} from {{ fy }}. These are mostly federal and state grants and money set aside for a named purpose. Council does not choose how most of it is spent. See why this site starts with the General Fund.

{% if other.split %}{{ m.split_bar(other.split, "Other funds income in the " ~ py ~ " proposal, by source", unit="share") }}{% endif %}

Read these figures with care. Other funds are budgeted at the most the city might receive. Actual receipts are usually far lower. And the books' actual figures for other funds are incomplete for 2010 to 2014 and 2017 to 2022, so the chart leaves those years out.
